Factors That Influence Your Timeline

Your personal experience may differ based on:

• Dose titration schedule• Other medications you're taking• Hydration status• Diet composition (fatty or spicy foods can trigger nausea)• Underlying conditions (e.g., gastrointestinal disorders)

Practical Tips to Ease Side Effects

Dose Titration

Follow your doctor's schedule for gradual increases.

Slower titration often means fewer side effects.

Diet Modifications

Eat small, frequent meals.

Avoid high-fat, greasy or very spicy foods.

Include bland options: crackers, plain rice, toast.

Hydration

Sip water or clear liquids throughout the day.

Electrolyte solutions can help if you're experiencing diarrhea.

Timing of Injection

Try taking your injection with the largest meal (if your prescriber allows).

Some people find evening injections reduce daytime nausea.

Over-the-Counter Remedies

Antacids or antiemetics (e.g., ondansetron) may help—check with your doctor first.

Fiber supplements can ease constipation.

Lifestyle Adjustments

Light exercise (walking, gentle yoga) may reduce nausea.

Stress-management techniques (deep breathing, meditation).
