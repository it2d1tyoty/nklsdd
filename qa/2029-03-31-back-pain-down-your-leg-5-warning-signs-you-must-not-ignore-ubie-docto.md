Explanation

[Warning Signs You Must](https://memorycircuits.hbsnzj.com/blog/1933937069.html)

When you feel back pain that travels down your leg, it could be a sign of a condition called sciatica. This happens because something is pressing on the nerves in your lower back. The nerves in your back go all the way down your legs, so if they are squeezed or irritated, you might feel pain in your legs.

What Causes Sciatica?

Spinal Issues: Sometimes, problems with the spine like a bulging disc or vertebrae issues can cause sciatica. These problems can press on the nerves, causing pain to shoot down your leg.

Other Factors: Other factors like injury or certain activities might also cause sciatica.
