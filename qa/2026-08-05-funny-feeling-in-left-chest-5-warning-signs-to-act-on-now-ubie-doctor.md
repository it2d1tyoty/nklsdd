Chest discomfort can have many causes. Sometimes, heart issues like angina, which is when the heart does not get enough blood, can create chest pain. Other times, problems with the esophagus cause pain. For example, if a pill irritates the lining of your esophagus (a problem called pill-induced esophagitis), you might feel a funny sensation on the left side of your chest.

[Chest Warning Signs to](https://tendonforge.dns0663.com/blog/3832608602.html)

In one case report, ascorbic acid (Vitamin C) pills were linked to such irritation. Chest pain can also be seen after using substances like cocaine, which may cause changes in blood flow to the heart. Additionally, chest wall pain from strained muscles or inflammation of the cartilage (costochondritis) is another possibility.
