How does erythritol affect blood clotting?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Erythritol is a popular sugar substitute found in many low-carb and keto foods, but recent studies suggest it may be linked to a higher risk of blood clots, heart attack, and stroke. Researchers found that elevated erythritol levels in the blood can make platelets more likely to clot, potentially raising cardiovascular risk in susceptible individuals.
