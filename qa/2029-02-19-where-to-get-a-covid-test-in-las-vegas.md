Covid Tests From Local Clinics

There are four local clinics in Las Vegas that offer Covid testing.

The Southern Nevada Health District is open for Covid testing Monday through Friday from 6 a.m. to noon. Appointments are recommended, but walk-ins are welcome. Covid vaccines are also available at this location.

[Las Vegas](https://pigmentlab.jcesqc8.com/)

Nevada Partners offers free Covid testing on the second and fourth Wednesday of each week from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. No appointment is required. They also offer Covid vaccines.

OptimuMedicine offers Covid tests every Sunday through Friday from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. Appointments are required. Testing is free at this location. No vaccines are available here.

The College of Southern Nevada Charleston Campus offers Covid testing every Monday and Tuesday from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. and every Wednesday through Friday from noon to 4 p.m. Appointments are required. Testing is free, but there are no Covid vaccines offered.

DIY rapid tests are available from pharmacies. Walgreens and CVS both offer home test kits.
