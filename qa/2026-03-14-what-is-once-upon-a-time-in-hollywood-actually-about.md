# What Is 'Once Upon a Time in Hollywood' Actually About?

The trailer for Quentin Tarantino’s new film, Once Upon a Time in Hollywood, has dropped. This is the director’s first movie since 2015’s The Hateful Eight, and also his first release since cutting all ties with The Weinstein Company. The trailer provides a glimpse of Tarantino’s trademark style, and a mish-mash of fact and fiction which will be familiar to fans of his earlier film Inglourious Basterds.

Here’s what we know about Once Upon a Time in Hollywood so far, including details on the synopsis—and what it has to do with Sharon Tate and Charles Manson.

What's the plot of Once Upon a Time in Hollywood?

Once Upon a Time in Hollywood incorporates original characters invented by Tarantino into one of the most infamous true crime cases of the 20th century.

Set in 1969, the film follows fictitious actor Rick Dalton and his stunt double Cliff Booth, whose quest to make it big in the film industry becomes increasingly entangled in the real life events which led up to the murder of actress Sharon Tate by the followers of Charles Manson. Within the narrative of the movie, the made-up Dalton lives next door to the real Tate, a circumstance which results in him crossing paths with some of the most famous figures of the time.

“I’ve been working on this script for five years, as well as living in Los Angeles most of my life, including in 1969, when I was seven years old,” Tarantino said in a statement. “I’m very excited to tell this story of an LA and a Hollywood that don’t exist anymore.”

Who's in the cast?

The washed up Rick Dalton is played by Leonardo DiCaprio, while Brad Pitt plays his stunt double and long-time friend, Cliff Booth. “Actors are required to do a lot of dangerous stuff. Cliff here is meant to help carry the load,” DiCaprio’s character says in the trailer, to which Pitt’s Cliff replies: “What, carrying his load? Yeah, that’s about right.”

Sharon Tate, the actress whose very real murder by the Manson family forms a major plot point in the film, is played by Margot Robbie. Dakota Fanning plays Lynette “Squeaky” Fromme, a member of the Manson family, and Emile Hirsch plays Jay Sebring, a Hollywood hairstylist who was another one of their victims. Manson himself is played by Damon Herriman, who previously played the same man in Netflix original series Mindhunter.

The trailer revealed that a number of Hollywood’s late icons will be making cameos in the movie, including Mike Moh as Bruce Lee, and Damian Lewis as Steve McQueen.
