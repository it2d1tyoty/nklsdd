Why does excessive burping often occur at night?

Reviewed by Suo Lee, MD

Internal Medicine

Excessive burping at night can be caused by swallowing air during the day, which is released when lying down. Sleep can affect how much you burp.

Explanation

Excessive burping, especially at night, can be uncomfortable. When you swallow air during the day, it can get trapped in your stomach. At night, when you lie down, this air can come out as burps.

Why It Happens:

Swallowing Air: During the day, you might swallow air while eating, drinking, or talking. This is called aerophagia.

Position: Lying down can make it easier for the air to come up from your stomach.
