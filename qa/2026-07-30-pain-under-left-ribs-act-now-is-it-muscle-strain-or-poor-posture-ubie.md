Can muscle strain or poor posture cause pain under the left ribs?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Muscle strain and poor posture are common causes of pain under the left ribs. Athletes, people who lift heavy objects, and those with muscle tears in the chest wall, intercostal muscles, or upper abdomen often experience this discomfort. Slouching, prolonged sitting, or sudden twisting movements can also inflame surrounding muscles and lead to sharp or aching pain beneath the left rib cage.

While musculoskeletal issues are frequent culprits, pain under the left ribs can also signal other conditions involving the spleen, stomach, pancreas, or heart. Because the causes vary widely, it's important to identify what's driving your symptoms before assuming it's muscle-related. Explanation

Pain under the left ribs can indeed be caused by muscle strain or poor posture. According to the medical literature, musculoskeletal problems of the chest wall, such as those experienced by athletes, can lead to pain in this area. Poor posture can place additional stress on the muscles around the ribs, potentially leading to discomfort or pain.

Muscle Strain: A side strain, which involves the tearing of the internal oblique muscles, can cause significant pain under the ribs. This type of injury is often seen in athletes and can result from sudden movements or overuse.
