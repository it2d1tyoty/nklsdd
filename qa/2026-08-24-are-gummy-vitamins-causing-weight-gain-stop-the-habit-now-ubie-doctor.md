Can eating too many vitamin gummies contribute to weight gain?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Eating too many vitamin gummies is unlikely to directly cause weight gain, as most contain minimal calories and sugar per serving. However, overconsumption can pose serious health risks, including vitamin toxicity, digestive issues, liver damage, and nutrient imbalances—especially with fat-soluble vitamins like A, D, E, and K that accumulate in the body.

If you've been overdoing it on vitamin gummies and are experiencing symptoms like nausea, fatigue, headaches, or digestive discomfort, it's important to understand what's happening in your body.
