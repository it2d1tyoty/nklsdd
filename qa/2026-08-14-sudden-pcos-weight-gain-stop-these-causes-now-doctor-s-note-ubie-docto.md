Sudden weight gain in individuals with PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) can happen because of several reasons related to hormones

Hormonal Changes

Insulin Resistance: This can make your body store more fat.

Increased Androgens: These are male hormones that can lead to weight gain.

Other Factors

Midlife Changes: As women get older, their bodies change, which can also cause weight gain.
