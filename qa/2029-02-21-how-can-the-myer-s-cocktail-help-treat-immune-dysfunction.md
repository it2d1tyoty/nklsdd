What is immune dysfunction?

Immune dysfunction refers to various conditions in which the immune system malfunctions. These can manifest as an overactive immune response, which can lead to allergies and autoimmune diseases, or an underactive immune system, which renders the body vulnerable to infections and diseases.

Symptoms can range from frequent infections and slow wound healing to chronic inflammation, fatigue, and digestive issues. Due to the vast range of symptoms, self-diagnosing may prove challenging, so you should consult a medical professional if you think you may have immune dysfunction.

What causes immune dysfunction?

The causes of immune dysfunction can vary significantly from person to person. They can include genetic factors, age, stress levels, inadequate nutrition, chronic diseases such as diabetes, and medication use.

For many people, the underlying causes of immune dysfunction can relate to gaps in their nutrition or a deficiency. When the body lacks essential nutrients for a prolonged period, this can result in compromises within the immune system.
