Lifestyle Changes: Reducing caffeine and alcohol intake can help, as they may irritate the bladder.

Pelvic Floor Exercises: These exercises strengthen the muscles that control urination and can reduce symptoms of frequency.

Medications: Depending on the underlying cause, medications might be prescribed to help manage symptoms.

Understanding the Cause: It is important to identify the specific cause of frequent urination to determine the most effective treatment. Consulting with a healthcare provider can help tailor the management plan to individual needs.
