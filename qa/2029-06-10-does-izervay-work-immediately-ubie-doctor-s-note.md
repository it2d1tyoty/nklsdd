Explanation

Izervay, also known as avacincaptad pegol, is a treatment to help slow vision loss in patients with geographic atrophy rather than to fix vision right away. After you receive an injection, the medicine begins working at a cellular level to help protect and preserve your vision. However, you will not notice a quick or immediate improvement in how you see. Instead, over many months of treatment, Izervay can reduce the speed at which vision loss happens. This treatment works gradually by targeting processes in the eye that lead to damage. It is similar to some other medicines where the benefit is seen by the long-term slowing of a disease’s progress. Because of this gradual effect, it is very important to follow up with your eye doctor regularly during treatment so they can monitor your condition and adjust care as needed. Remember, if you have any questions about your treatment or if you’re not sure how it is working, you should talk with your healthcare provider.
