Herbal supplements such as valerian and chamomile can interact with prescription medications, potentially causing harmful side effects or reducing how well your medications work. Common risks include increased drowsiness when combined with sedatives, bleeding risks with blood thinners, and altered absorption of other drugs. Always consult your doctor or pharmacist before combining herbal remedies with prescription treatments.
