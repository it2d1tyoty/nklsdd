Stage 3 liver disease, also called advanced fibrosis, involves significant scarring of the liver tissue. While it is not always fully reversible, progression can often be slowed or partially improved through targeted lifestyle changes, treating the underlying cause (such as hepatitis, fatty liver disease, or alcohol use), and taking prescribed medications. Early intervention offers the best chance of preventing progression to cirrhosis.

Because liver disease symptoms often overlap with other conditions and can be subtle, it's important to identify what's driving them as early as possible.
