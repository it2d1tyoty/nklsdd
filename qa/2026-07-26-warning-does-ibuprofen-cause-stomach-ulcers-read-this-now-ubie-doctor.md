# Warning: Does Ibuprofen Cause Stomach Ulcers? Read This Now! | Ubie Doctor's Note

Does ibuprofen cause stomach ulcers?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

[This Now Ubie](https://hostdefense-journal.escortskhi.com/)

Ibuprofen can cause stomach ulcers, particularly with frequent use, high doses, or long-term treatment. As a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID), ibuprofen reduces protective prostaglandins in the stomach lining, increasing the risk of irritation, bleeding, and ulcer formation. Risk is higher in older adults, people with a history of ulcers, those taking blood thinners or steroids, and individuals who consume alcohol regularly.

Stomach pain, nausea, or dark stools after taking ibuprofen shouldn't be ignored. Since ulcer symptoms can overlap with many other conditions, identifying the cause early matters.
