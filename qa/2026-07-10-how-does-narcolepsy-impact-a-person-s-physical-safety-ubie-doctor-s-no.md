Wondering how narcolepsy affects safety?

Explanation

Narcolepsy is a sleep disorder that makes people feel very sleepy during the day. This can make it hard for them to stay awake and alert.

How It Affects Safety:

Driving: Falling asleep while driving is very dangerous and can cause accidents.

Cooking: If someone falls asleep while cooking, it can lead to fires.

Using Machines: Falling asleep while using tools or machines can cause injuries.
