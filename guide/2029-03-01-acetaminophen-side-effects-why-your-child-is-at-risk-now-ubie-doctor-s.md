Children can experience acetaminophen side effects differently than adults, with dosage errors and toxicity risks being especially concerning. Because a child's liver processes the medication differently, even small overdoses may cause serious harm. Common pediatric side effects include nausea, vomiting, rash, and stomach pain, while more severe reactions can involve liver damage or allergic responses.

Recognizing symptoms early is critical to keeping your child safe.
