Foods to Eat After Diarrhea: A Quick Recovery Guide

After diarrhea, focus on bland, easily digestible foods and proper hydration to help your gut recover. The BRAT diet — bananas, rice, applesauce, and toast — is widely recommended because these foods are low in fiber, gentle on the stomach, and help firm up stools. Other good options include boiled potatoes, plain crackers, oatmeal, and clear broths. Replenish lost fluids and electrolytes with water, oral rehydration solutions, or diluted juice. Avoid dairy, spicy foods, caffeine, and fatty meals until you fully recover.

Still, diarrhea can stem from many causes — from mild viral infections to food intolerances or more serious conditions. Knowing the underlying reason matters, because the right recovery plan depends on it.
