What lifestyle changes can help manage Obstructive Sleep Apnea?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Losing weight, eating a healthy diet, and exercising regularly are proven lifestyle changes that can help manage Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA). Excess weight—especially around the neck—can narrow the airway and worsen breathing interruptions during sleep. Even a modest weight loss of 5–10% may significantly reduce OSA severity, improve sleep quality, and lower related health risks like high blood pressure and heart disease.

Healthy Eating: Eating a balanced diet with fewer calories can help you lose weight and may improve OSA.
