Can skin boosters go wrong?

While rare, adverse effects like overfilling, uneven results, or inflammation can occur if injected improperly. This is why choosing a trained and experienced injector is crucial.

Is Jalupro approved by the FDA?

As of now, Jalupro is CE-marked for use in Europe but not FDA-approved in the United States. However, it is widely used by practitioners in many countries with strong safety records.

Is Jalupro better than Profhilo?

Jalupro vs Profhilo is a common question, and the simplest answer is that each has its strengths. Jalupro includes peptides and amino acids, offering more regenerative action. Profhilo is a pure HA hydrator. The best option depends on the skin condition and treatment goal.
