# Bodybuilder Simeon Panda Broke Down How He Built His Digital Fitness Empire

[Down How](https://allpepay.com/blog/3332231099.html)

In the latest episode of his webseries Don't Break, powerlifter and trainer C.T. Fletcher hit the gym with Simeon Panda, the British bodybuilder with more than 6 million Instagram followers who was ranked in the Top 10 fitness influencers of the world by Forbes. As the two of them worked their way through a weights training session, Panda opened up about his love for fitness, and how he was able to turn that passion into a career through his online coaching and Just Lift brand.

"The social media side started in 2010, 2011," he explained. "I'd already been training 10 years by that time, so I thought, let me share what I know. The first thing I ever did was put out some blog posts on a website which was basically dispelling myths, the basic things that people who were new to the gym wouldn't know. I just put them out there, I didn't expect anything from it, I just put it out, and people responded."

"I took the time to do ebooks," he continued, "I thought it they're taking these blog posts and responding to it, maybe I'll package everything into an ebook and see how that does. And that was pretty much the start of everything. It made me think that if I pursue this full-time, where can I take it? And eventually I took that leap, and I left my 9-to-5."

Despite bodybuilding and fitness now being an all-consuming full-time job, Panda still enjoys it as much as ever.

"Obsession is normally not a good thing," he said. "It's more passion. I'm methodical with it. It's not like a mindless obsession, there's a method to what I'm doing. When I take a vacation, I need to know where I'm training. But I also put the caveat that it's not because I'm obsessed with it, it's because I enjoy training, and so a holiday is about having a good time. I will have a good time as long as I can train."

As his online following grew, and he continued to receive messages and feedback from people telling him what a difference he had made to their lives, Panda said that he felt increasingly motivated to carry on. And as far as the inevitable trolls are concerned, he pays them no mind.

"Keep doing good," he said, "and inspire the people who want to be inspired."
