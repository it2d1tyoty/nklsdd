# The Rock Opens Up About Watching His Daughter's Birth

[Up About](https://synaptide-lab.snaketongueofficial.com/)

Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson is all about his family, and the proof is all over the Internet. From his amazing Mother's Day posts for his girlfriend Lauren Hashian and mom Ata Johnson, to the sweet photo of him feeding Hashian as she breastfed their newborn daughter Tiana, it seems like there are no bounds to Johnson's status as a doting dad, son, and partner.

[His Daughter's Birth](https://calmvector.dns0663.com/)

Now, the Skyscraper star is opening up about watching Hashian give birth. "I will say, it was the most amazing experience I’ve ever seen," Johnson said during a recent visit to Live With Kelly and Ryan. "I look at Lauren, and women and mothers, in a different light than I ever have before. And it was beautiful."

Johnson also has two older daughters ― 16-year-old daughter Simone, who had with ex-wife Dany Garcia, and 2-year-old Jasmine, his first daughter with Hashian. However, as People reports, it was only with the birth of his third daughter that he stood below the drape.

[Opens Up About](https://calmvector.dns0663.com/blog/0562789809.html)

"I was encouraging her — holding her hand. And the doctor said, ‘Would you like to come down and watch and help?’" Johnson said. "I said, ‘Yup, I’m going to come down. Watch. Help.’"

"I’m holding legs, everything. And out comes the baby," he continued. "I know, right? Crazy. ‘Cause you roll the dice. You don’t know how it’s going go to go. A lot of stuff happens."

When Johnson first shared the news of Tiana's birth on Instagram in April, he also commented on how powerful it was to watch his girlfriend give birth.

"Word to the wise gentlemen, it’s critical to be by your lady’s head when she’s delivering, being as supportive as you can.. holding hands, holding legs, whatever you can do," he wrote. "But, if you really want to understand the single most powerful and primal moment life will ever offer - watch your child being born. Its a life changer and the respect and admiration you have for a woman, will forever be boundless."
