FOR MEN'S summer wardrobe essentials, there’s perhaps no staple that’s as ready for all those balmy days as a camp collar shirt. Also known as a Cuban collar shirt, these laid-back, easy-to-wear button-up short-sleeve shirts make an impression through their signature collar that lays flat against your upper chest to create a V-shaped neckline. Truly, the best camp shirts are as ready for vacation as they're for daily life.

Even though they carry a degree of nonchalance—which is part of what makes them so appealing—camp collars are so versatile that it’s almost impossible to imagine how not to wear them. And thanks to their lightweight fabrics like linen, silk, and cotton—coupled with that flat, open collar—they’re uncomplicated and untucked, extra airy and uber-cozy even on a scorching day. So if you want to survive summer’s sizzling day, camp collar shirts are a must for your rotation.

How to Wear a Camp Collar Shirt

Fit: Part of the casual sartorial breed, even a true-to-size camp collar shirt can look a bit relaxed-fitting and oversized, but that’s okay—that’s in fact the vibe you want to sport. However, if you’ve got a brawny build, it’s also good to size up for camp shirts. After all, as summer shirts, you’ll want some room for the air and breathing.

Color/Pattern: Solid tone is always a, well, solid choice for any day, anywhere. However, as summer shirts, you’re allowed to go wild and creative with prints and patterns—so long as they make sense. Tropical-inspired or floral prints are a failsafe for more casual occasions like vacationing. Subtle textures work for more elevated times.

Bottoms: Pairing camp shirts with any sort of summer shorts—chinos, denim if the color works, athletic, and even swim trunks—can’t go wrong. But don’t go tucking them in your shorts unless you’ve got the build to show off. Pants, on the other hand, also work well with camp shirts because of the contrast—“boxy relaxed top over slender fitted bottoms” kind of contrast. In fact, with pants, you can tuck your Cuban collar shirts. Whether it’s chinos, jeans, or dress trousers, so long they work well in tones, they’ll work. However, be mindful of sweat shorts and sweatpants: if they look way too lounge-y and fail to harmonize in hues, they’ll look totally out of place with your camp collar top.
