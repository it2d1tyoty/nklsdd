Internal Medicine

A swollen uvula (uvulitis) is typically treated with corticosteroids to reduce inflammation and antibiotics if a bacterial infection is the cause. Supportive care—such as staying hydrated, gargling with salt water, using throat lozenges, and taking over-the-counter pain relievers—can also ease symptoms. In severe cases where breathing is affected, emergency medical treatment may be required.

Because a swollen uvula can stem from many different causes—including infections, allergies, acid reflux, dehydration, or trauma—the right treatment depends on identifying the underlying trigger. Guessing wrong could delay relief or lead to complications. Keeping the throat moist and staying hydrated can help alleviate symptoms.

Avoiding irritants like smoking or allergens may prevent further irritation.
