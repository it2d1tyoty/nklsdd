Migraine Disorders: Chronic vs. Episodic, Triptans, and Preventive Care

Calcium-based antacids are generally considered safe, while H2 blockers or PPIs may be used under medical guidance if symptoms persist. Other important considerations include knowing which supplements to avoid, recognizing warning symptoms that require urgent care, and using supportive habits like left-side sleeping and stress management. Most pregnancy-related GERD resolves after delivery. Because symptoms can overlap with more serious conditions, understanding what's driving your discomfort is key.
