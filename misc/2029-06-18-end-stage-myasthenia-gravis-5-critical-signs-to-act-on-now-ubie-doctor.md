End-stage myasthenia gravis (MG) is the most advanced phase of this autoimmune neuromuscular disorder, marked by severe, life-threatening muscle weakness. Key symptoms include profound difficulty breathing (myasthenic crisis), impaired swallowing, and complications such as respiratory failure, aspiration pneumonia, and renal failure. At this stage, patients often require long-term mechanical ventilation and intensive supportive care.

Because MG symptoms can escalate quickly and mimic other conditions, early recognition is critical. Severe Muscle Weakness: Patients experience significant difficulty in moving and performing daily activities.

Breathing Problems: Because the muscles that help with breathing are weak, patients may struggle to breathe on their own.
