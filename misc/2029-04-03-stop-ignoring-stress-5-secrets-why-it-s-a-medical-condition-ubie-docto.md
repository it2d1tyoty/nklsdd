5 Anxiety Secrets: Why Your "Stress" Might Be a Medical Condition

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Anxiety is more than everyday stress—it's a recognized medical condition that affects the entire body and can be triggered or worsened by treatable causes such as thyroid disease, heart rhythm problems, low blood sugar, asthma, nutrient deficiencies, or hormonal shifts.

Identifying the underlying cause and pattern is key to choosing the right next step. Anxiety is highly manageable through evaluation, therapy, medication, and lifestyle changes. However, seek urgent care for red flags like chest pain, fainting, sudden shortness of breath, new confusion or weakness, or thoughts of self-harm.

Secret #5: Ignoring Anxiety Symptoms Can Delay Serious Diagnoses

Most Anxiety symptoms are not dangerous on their own—but some symptoms should never be ignored, especially if they are new, severe, or worsening.

Seek medical attention urgently or speak to a doctor right away if Anxiety-like symptoms include:

Chest pain or pressure

Fainting or loss of consciousness

Sudden shortness of breath

New confusion or weakness

Thoughts of self-harm or hopelessness

Doctors are trained to tell the difference between Anxiety and conditions that can be life-threatening. It is always appropriate to ask for help if something feels wrong.

Avoiding care out of fear of "overreacting" can be more harmful than seeking reassurance.
