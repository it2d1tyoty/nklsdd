What is Phentermine Prescribed For?

Phentermine is a prescription medicine used to reduce appetite and support weight loss. It is typically used as part of a comprehensive weight-loss plan developed by your weight loss doctor. Patients taking phentermine will also need to incorporate healthy eating, physical activity, and other behavior changes. Phentermine is a Schedule 4 drug, indicating a potential for misuse, though the rate of misuse is low.
