Explanation

Glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) medications are used to help with weight loss and managing blood sugar. Some studies suggest that these medications might cause a decrease in muscle mass, also known as lean body mass.

Research discusses changes in lean body mass with GLP-1 use, but often does not provide specific percentages of users experiencing muscle loss. Studies examining the effects of GLP-1 receptor agonists also do not always specify how many people lose muscle. Reviews of the impact of specific GLP-1 medications, like semaglutide, on lean mass typically do not give a precise percentage of muscle loss.

While these references discuss the potential for muscle loss with GLP-1 medications, they do not specify how many people are affected.
