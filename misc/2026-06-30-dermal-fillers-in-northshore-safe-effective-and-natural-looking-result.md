What Are Dermal Fillers?

Dermal fillers are injectable treatments that help restore volume to the face, smooth wrinkles, and enhance contours. These fillers are made of various substances, including hyaluronic acid (HA), calcium hydroxylapatite (CaHA), and poly-L-lactic acid (PLLA). The most commonly used dermal fillers, such as HA-based fillers, provide immediate results that improve over time.
