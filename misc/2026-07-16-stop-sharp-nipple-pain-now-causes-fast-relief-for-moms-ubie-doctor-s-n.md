What could cause sharp nipple pain while breastfeeding, and how can it be managed?

Sharp nipple pain during breastfeeding is often a symptom of Raynaud's phenomenon, a condition where blood vessels in the nipple constrict, causing pain, blanching, and color changes. Effective management typically includes keeping the nipples warm before and after feeding, avoiding cold exposure, applying dry heat, and, in some cases, using prescribed medications such as nifedipine to improve blood flow.
