To all you aspiring raisins: Don't buy into tanning-salon claims that gradual UV exposure is the answer to sunburns. A tan is, in fact, worse than a sunburn, according to Norman Levine, M.D., a professor of dermatology at the University of Arizona. A burn tells you to get out of the sun, whereas a painless tan tricks you into staying out for aesthetic purposes.

The best way to build some color without compromising your health is to check out self-tanning products containing the FDA-approved skin-tinting solution dihydroxyacetone, or DHA. Made up of aloe vera, vitamins, and natural bronzers, the substance binds to proteins in your skin and turns them brown, according to Rhonda Venuto, a spokeswoman for Hollywood Tans--where a $25 session in a spray-on tanning booth allows you to apply that very solution evenly in 6 seconds without the DIY mess.

But beware. "It's a safe way to produce a stain, but it's not going to protect you from the sun," says Dr. Levine. For that, you need a sunscreen with SPF 15 or higher.
