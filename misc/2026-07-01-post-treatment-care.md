After Your Appointment

Keep the treated area clean and dry for the remainder of the day.

Apply a cool compress gently to reduce swelling or tenderness as needed. Do not apply ice directly to the skin.

Sleep on your back with your head elevated the first night, if possible.

Resume normal skincare later the same day using light pressure only, avoiding treated areas.

Stay well hydrated unless otherwise instructed by your provider.

Avoid NSAIDs (ibuprofen) and take Acetaminophen (Tylenol) for any discomfort

Your provider may direct you to apply Arnica gel to the site of injection and/or take an antihistamine (Claritin, Zyrtec)

Please Avoid

For the first 24 hours:

Touching, rubbing, or massaging the treated areas unless directed by your provider

Strenuous exercise or activities that increase heart rate significantly

Alcohol consumption

Excessive heat exposure (saunas, steam rooms, hot tubs)

What to Expect

[Post Treatment Care](https://jointrevive.xianfengchem.com/)

Swelling, redness, tenderness, firmness, or asymmetry are common and temporary

Bruising may occur and typically resolves within 7–14 days

Fillers may feel firm initially and will soften as they integrate

Final results are typically visible once swelling has fully resolved

Individual results vary based on product used, anatomy, injection technique, and adherence to post-care instructions.

When to Contact the Office Immediately

Please contact the office promptly or seek urgent medical care if you experience:

Increasing pain, swelling, redness, or warmth after 48 hours

Signs of infection such as fever or discharge
