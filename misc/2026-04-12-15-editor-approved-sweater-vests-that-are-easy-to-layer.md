SOMEWHERE BETWEEN Mike Ditka and Ryan Gosling, the sweater vest got a bad rap. However, if you look back at old pictures of well-dressed guys from the early twentieth century, you'll see that sweater vests have always been stylish. Then in 1985, Ditka sported the Chicago Bears sweater vests, one of the most infamous coaching uniforms of all time. Since then, the humble sweater vests became associated with staid college professors and even blander golf fits.

That all changed a few years back when Ryan Gosling and a few more stylish leading started rocking sweater vests, steering them back in fashion. Legacy menswear brands blew the dust off of their vintage sweater vest designs while new, forward-thinking labels updated the concept with a streetwear flair (think: collegiate piping, thicker knits). Then Harry Styles joined the sweater vest party by way of Gucci, and the classic garment has never looked cooler.

Flash forward to 2023 and the best sweater vests for men have become a winter wardrobe all star. They're easy to throw under your work blazer for extra warmth and style points, and they look just as cool without a jacket once temps start to rise.
