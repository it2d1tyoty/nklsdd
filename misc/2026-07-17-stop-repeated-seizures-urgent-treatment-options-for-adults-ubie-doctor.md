Repeated seizures in adults are typically treated with antiepileptic drugs (AEDs) and benzodiazepines. AEDs help prevent future seizures, while benzodiazepines are often used to stop active or cluster seizures quickly. The right treatment depends on seizure type, frequency, and overall health, so working closely with a healthcare provider is essential to find the safest and most effective option.

Medications:

Benzodiazepines: These are often used for quick control of seizures. They can be given in various forms, such as orally or as a nasal spray.

Antiepileptic Drugs (AEDs): Long-term control of seizures may involve daily medications to prevent seizures from happening.
