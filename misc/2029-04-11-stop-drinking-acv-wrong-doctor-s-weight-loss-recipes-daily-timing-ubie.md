What are some recipes for apple cider vinegar weight loss drinks, and how often should they be consumed?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Apple cider vinegar (ACV) may support weight loss when consumed daily in small amounts, typically 1–2 tablespoons diluted in water or mixed into foods like salad dressings. Some studies suggest ACV may help reduce appetite, lower blood sugar spikes, and modestly decrease body fat. However, evidence remains limited, and more research is needed to confirm its effectiveness and long-term safety.
