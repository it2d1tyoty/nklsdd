Pleural effusion treatment focuses on draining excess fluid from the chest cavity to relieve pressure on the lungs and restore normal breathing. Common procedures include thoracentesis (needle drainage), chest tube insertion, or pleurodesis for recurring cases. Treating the underlying cause—such as infection, heart failure, or cancer—is also essential for long-term recovery.

Pleural effusion is when fluid builds up in the space around the lungs. This can make it hard to breathe. Treatment Options:

Thoracentesis: This is a procedure where a needle is used to take out the fluid.

Chest Tube Drainage: In more serious cases, a tube might be placed in the chest to keep draining the fluid over time.
