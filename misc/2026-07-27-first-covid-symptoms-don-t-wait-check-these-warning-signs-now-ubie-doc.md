Explanation

Fatigue, fever, cough, sore throat, and headache are often seen initially. However, there are cases where changes in taste or smell may also occur first. Furthermore, fever does not necessarily come first. Sometimes the initial symptoms are just sore throat or headache. If you are concerned about your symptoms, you can check them with a free AI symptom checker to help determine your next steps, then wear a mask and get tested.
