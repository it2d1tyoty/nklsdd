Understanding NMN Heat Sensitivity

NMN is particularly sensitive to heat and moisture. Exposure to high temperatures or humidity can cause the molecule to degrade, turning an active supplement into an ineffective powder. Key points:

At temperatures above 25 °C (77 °F), NMN begins to break down more quickly.

Prolonged exposure to heat accelerates degradation, sometimes within days or weeks.

Humidity and light further speed up this process.

When your NMN degrades, you may be taking a supplement that does little more than fill a capsule.

Best Practices for NMN Storage

Here's how to store NMN supplements to guard against heat sensitivity and maintain potency:

Store in a cool, dark place:• Aim for a temperature between 2 °C and 8 °C (35.6 °F–46.4 °F).• A refrigerator's crisper drawer or a dedicated cooler bag works well.

Keep away from moisture:• Use desiccant packets included in many supplement bottles.• Ensure the bottle cap is tight after each use.

Avoid direct sunlight:• Ultraviolet (UV) light can speed up molecular breakdown.• Store in opaque containers or inside a closed cabinet.

Minimize air exposure:• Air can introduce moisture and oxygen, both of which degrade NMN.• Consider transferring capsules or powder into smaller, airtight vials if you don't plan to use the entire bottle at once.

Signs Your NMN May Have Degraded

Even with careful storage, it's wise to check your supplements regularly. Warning signs include:

Discoloration: A yellow or brown hue instead of white or off-white.

Clumping or caking: Indicates moisture exposure.

Unusual odor: Could signal microbial growth or chemical breakdown.

Reduced effects: You may notice less energy, mental clarity, or recovery support than expected.

If you spot any of these signs, discontinue use and replace the product.

Potential Risks of Improperly Stored NMN

While NMN itself is generally well tolerated, degraded supplements may carry risks:

Inconsistent dosing• Potency may vary capsule to capsule, making it hard to track results.

Your doctor's caution aims to prevent these pitfalls and keep you safe.
