What's That Equipment He's Using?

The machine Stallone’s using for his two exercises is one not too frequently found in a gym: a paddle/swim ergometer. If you can’t tell by watching, Stallone’s getting a killer shoulder, back, and chest workout in.

If you want to recreate a similar burn but don’t have this machine, try it out with a couple of resistance bands.
