What to Look for

Types of Cowboy Boots

When shopping for your cowboy boots, the first question to ask yourself is what type of Western boot do you want? Traditional cowboy boots usually have a taller shaft, while ropers veer on the shorter side. Then, of course, there are work boots—which you might associate with everyday ranchers—and dress boots, which are the more common option for those who want a stylish addition to their wardrobes.

Toe Shapes

Then, there are the toe shapes. Do you want a square toe, a standard round, or something on the pointer, almond shape? This will depend on your personal tastes, but it's important to keep in mind when you're comparing different options for your next purchase.

Material

Finally, there's the material. More often than not, you'll be looking at cowboy boots with different types of leather or suede. The type will determine how soft the boots will feel. If you're feeling fancy, you can always opt for exotic leathers like ostrich or alligator skin. However, these will typically run on the pricier side, so keep that in mind.
