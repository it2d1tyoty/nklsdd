The brand's Transitional Sub Flannel and Sur Sweatshirt, for example, are regulars on our Best Men's Flannels and Best Crewneck Sweatshirts lists, respectively.

Outerknown's flagship product, the blanket shirt, is so damn cozy that it needs its own national holiday. Fortunately, it basically already does—at least, in our eyes.

Outerknown's annual Blanket Shirt Day has officially been announced, and the one-day event will take place this Saturday, September 27. The blink-and-you-miss-it 24-hour sales event will feature insane savings on both Men’s and Women’s Blanket Shirts as well as its solid hue counterpart, the Chroma Blanket Shirt. Every regularly priced color shirt, which typically retails for $168 a pop, will be going for 2 for $250—nearly $100 in total savings.
