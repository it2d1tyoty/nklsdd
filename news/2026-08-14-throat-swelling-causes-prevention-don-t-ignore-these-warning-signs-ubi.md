What are the main causes of throat swelling and how can it be prevented?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Throat swelling is often caused by infections, ranging from mild illnesses like tonsillitis to serious conditions such as epiglottitis, which can block the airway and become life-threatening. Common causes include viral or bacterial infections, allergic reactions, and irritation from acid reflux or environmental triggers. Symptoms may include difficulty swallowing, hoarseness, muffled voice, drooling, or trouble breathing. Practicing good hygiene, staying up to date on vaccinations, avoiding known allergens, and seeking prompt medical care can significantly reduce your risk of complications.

Because throat swelling can stem from many different causes—some minor, others urgent—identifying the likely source early is critical.
