How effective is Opill at preventing pregnancy?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Opill is the first FDA-approved over-the-counter birth control pill available in the U.S. As a progestin-only contraceptive (also called the "mini-pill"), it is approximately 98% effective at preventing pregnancy when taken correctly—at the same time every day. Because Opill does not contain estrogen, it may be a suitable option for people who cannot take combination birth control pills.

Missing a dose or taking it more than three hours late can reduce its effectiveness, so consistency is essential. Common side effects may include irregular bleeding, headaches, nausea, and mood changes.
